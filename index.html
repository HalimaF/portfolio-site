About

I like owning the whole product: the database, the API, the screen, and the agent in the middle.

I am a final-year Computer Science student at Kinnaird College for Women in Lahore. I build and ship complete products: web platforms, mobile apps and agentic AI systems. My work runs across React, TypeScript, Flutter, Python, Flask, FastAPI, Node.js and SQL, and I use AI coding tools like Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot every day.

This summer I was an AI intern at Systems Limited, building agentic workflows that went into a live client system, with access controls so the agents could only take safe, approved actions. I am also co-founder of MartFlow, a retail commerce SaaS built on Odoo, and since 2021 I have freelanced with US clients on Amazon operations, automating their reporting with Python.

It catches duplicate invoices and bank-account-change fraud, does 2-way PO matching, and logs every decision with its confidence and reasoning.

    Python · LLM APIs · Multi-agent orchestration
